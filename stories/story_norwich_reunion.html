🇬🇧 Natural English Idiomatic Translation
After finally landing in London from Toronto, I was completely exhausted, but my spirits were high. I had to catch a train to Norwich today to reunite with Shivani. Shivani promised she'd be waiting for me right at the platform, since it's been a whole year since we last hung out.
Liverpool Street station was an absolute madhouse, and I found myself sprinting desperately toward platform nine. Spotting a ticket collector standing nearby, I called out, "Excuse me, does this train go straight to Norwich?" He gave a sly grin and said, "It sure does—now get a move on, the doors are about to shut!"
I scrambled inside, snagged an empty window seat, and immediately texted Shivani: "Made it on the train!" Ten minutes into the ride, an announcement crackled over the intercom: "Next stop: Cambridge!" My heart sank—turns out this was the split service, and I was going to have to scramble to switch trains at the next stop!
When I finally pulled into Norwich station, there was Shivani holding two takeaway coffees, already cracking up. "You flew all the way across the Atlantic from Toronto," she smirked, "and you still couldn't manage a simple direct train?!" Now we're heading straight to the pub to grab a pint and catch up on everything about her new job.
