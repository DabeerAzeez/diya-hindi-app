# Lesson A1-05: Habitual Past Tense & Past Continuous Tense


# 🌟Summary

Is lesson mein hum seekhenge ki past ki habits aur past mein chal rahe actions ko Hindi mein kaise bolte hain.

* **Habitual Past ("used to do" / "karta tha"):** Jab aap past ki routine ya repeated actions ki baat karte hain.
* **Past Continuous ("was doing" / "kar raha tha"):** Jab past ke kisi specific point par koi action ongoing ya continuous tha.
$$\text{Habitual Past: Verb Stem} + \text{-taa / -tee / -te} + \text{thaa / thee / the}$$

$$\text{Past Continuous: Verb Stem} + \text{rahaa / rahee / rahe} + \text{thaa / thee / the}$$

| **Tense** | **Masculine Singular** | **Feminine Singular** | **Plural / Respectful** | **Example (Karna - To do)** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Habitual Past** | Verb + *taa thaa* | Verb + *tee thee* | Verb + *te the* | *Main roz run karta thaa.* |
| **Past Continuous** | Verb + *rahaa thaa* | Verb + *rahee thee* | Verb + *rahe the* | *Main bike chala rahaa thaa.* |


# 📘Lesson


## ⏳ 1. Habitual Past Tense (Past Habits & Routines)

* Formula: `[Verb Root] + taa/tee/te + thaa/thee/the`
* English equivalent: "used to..." or "would always..."
* **Masculine Singular:** *Main bike se commute karta thaa.* (I used to commute by bike.)
* **Feminine Singular:** *Main guitar play kartee thee.* (I used to play guitar.)
* **Plural / Formal:** *Hum roz coffee peete the.* (We used to drink coffee every day.)

## 🔄 2. Past Continuous Tense (Ongoing Past Actions)

* Formula: `[Verb Root] + rahaa/rahee/rahe + thaa/thee/the`
* English equivalent: "was doing...", "were doing..."
* **Masculine Singular:** *Main code likh rahaa thaa.* (I was writing code.)
* **Feminine Singular:** *Woh music sun rahee thee.* (She was listening to music.)
* **Plural / Formal:** *Aap meeting attend kar rahe the.* (You were attending the meeting.)

## 🎯 3. Polyglot & Tamil Bridge

* **SOV Structure:** Dono tenses mein Subject pehle aayega, Object beech mein, aur conjugated Verb end mein.
* **Tamil Parallel:**
    * *Habitual Past:* Tamil ke "-vên / -thên" habit markers ki tarah Hindi mein verb root ke baad gender ending + past aux (*thaa/thee*) lagta hai.
    * *Past Continuous:* Tamil ke "-kittu irunthen" structure ki exact copy Hindi ka `[Verb] + rahaa thaa` hai.

# ❓Quiz


### Concept & Grammar Questions

* Habitual Past aur Past Continuous mein basic structural difference kya hai?
    * Habitual Past mein imperfective marker (*taa/tee/te*) lagta hai aur Past Continuous mein aspectual marker (*rahaa/rahee/rahe*) lagta hai, dono ke baad past auxiliary (*thaa/thee/the*) aata hai.
* Agar ek male speaker bol raha hai "I used to live here", toh ending kya hogi?
    * *Main yahan rehtaa thaa.* (Verb stem + *taa* + *thaa*).
* Past Continuous mein subject "Hum" (We) ke liye auxiliary aur aspect kya hoga?
    * Aspect *rahe* aur auxiliary *the* hoga (e.g., *Hum work kar rahe the*).
* "She was making coffee" ko translate karte waqt feminine markers kaise align honge?
    * Verb ke saath *rahee* aur auxiliary *thee* aayega: *Woh coffee bana rahee thee.*

### Translation Questions

* **I used to practice guitar every day.** *(speaker: m)*
    * `Main roz guitar practice kartaa thaa.`
    * Main (I) + roz (daily) + guitar (guitar) + practice kartaa thaa (used to practice)
* **I was working on a Python script yesterday.** *(speaker: m)*
    * `Main kal ek Python script par work kar rahaa thaa.`
    * Main (I) + kal (yesterday) + ek (a) + Python script par (on Python script) + work kar rahaa thaa (was working)
* **We were riding our bikes to the station.**
    * `Hum station tak bikes chala rahe the.`
    * Hum (We) + station tak (up to station) + bikes (bikes) + chala rahe the (were riding)
* **She used to speak French very well.**
    * `Woh French bohot achhi boltee thee.`
    * Woh (She) + French (French) + bohot achhi (very well) + boltee thee (used to speak)
* **What were you doing at 8 PM?** *(formal, m)*
    * `Aap raat ko 8 baje kya kar rahe the?`
    * Aap (You [formal]) + raat ko 8 baje (at 8 PM) + kya (what) + kar rahe the (were doing)
* **Hum pehle daily run karte the.**
    * We used to run daily before.
* **Woh office mein music mix kar rahaa thaa.**
    * He was mixing music in the office.
* **Tum kal kya study kar rahe the?**
    * What were you studying yesterday?
