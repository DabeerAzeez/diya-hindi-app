# Lesson A1-07: Quantifiers, Numbers, Shopping & Bargaining


# 🌟Summary

Is lesson mein hum master karenge shopping, prices poochna, quantifiers, aur authentic South Asian style mein bargaining karna! Chaahe aap Toronto mein new guitar gear ya synth cables dhoondh rahe hon, ya Shivani ke liye Norwich reunion trip se pehle market se unique gifts pick kar rahe hon—ye tools aapko instant conversational power aur street credibility dete hain.


### 🌐 Polyglot Mental Anchors:

* **Tamil Parallel:**
    * Quantifiers ekdam direct map hote hain: *Konjam* $\longleftrightarrow$ **Thodaa**, *Romba* $\longleftrightarrow$ **Bahut**, *Athigam / Niraiya* $\longleftrightarrow$ **Zyaadaa**, *Koraivu* $\longleftrightarrow$ **Kam**, *Ellaam* $\longleftrightarrow$ **Sab**.
    * Price inquiry: *Ithu evalavu?* $\longleftrightarrow$ **Ye kitne kaa hai?**
    * Demonstrative suffix *thu* (*intha* $\rightarrow$ *ithu*, *antha* $\rightarrow$ *anthathu*) bilkul Hindi ke **vaalaa / -vaalee / -vaale** (*ye vaalaa*, *vo vaalaa*) jaisa kaam karta hai!
* **Romance Parallel (French & Spanish):**
    * Demonstrative pronouns: *celui-ci / celui-là* (FR) aur *este / ese* (ES) $\longleftrightarrow$ **ye vaalaa / vo vaalaa**.
    * Asking price: *C'est combien?* (FR) / *¿Cuánto cuesta?* (ES) $\longleftrightarrow$ **Kitne kaa hai?**
    * Quantifiers: *trop / demasiado* $\longleftrightarrow$ **bahut zyaadaa**, *un peu / un poco* $\longleftrightarrow$ **thodaa**.

## 🔢 1. Practical Numbers: 1 to 30 & Key Tens

Hinglish mein English numbers bolna 100% accepted aur natural hai (*"Twenty dollars"*, *"Fifty pounds"*). Lekin local vendors, auto drivers, ya street markets mein quick prices samajhne aur bargaining counter-offers fekne ke liye 1–30 aur key tens pehchaanna zaroori hai:

| Range | Numbers (Romanized Hindi) |
| --- | --- |
| **1 – 10** | **Ek** (1), **Do** (2), **Teen** (3), **Chaar** (4), **Paanch** (5), **Chhah** (6), **Saat** (7), **Aath** (8), **Nau** (9), **Das** (10) |
| **11 – 20** | **Gyaarah** (11), **Baarah** (12), **Terah** (13), **Chaudah** (14), **Pandrah** (15), **Solah** (16), **Satrah** (17), **Athaarah** (18), **Unnees** (19), **Bees** (20) |
| **21 – 30** | **Ikkees** (21), **Baaees** (22), **Teyees** (23), **Chaubees** (24), **Pachchees** (25), **Chhabbees** (26), **Sattaayees** (27), **Athaayees** (28), **Untees** (29), **Tees** (30) |
| **Key Tens** | **Chaalees** (40), **Pachaas** (50), **Saath** (60), **Satthar** (70), **Assi** (80), **Nabbe** (90) |
| **Big Benchmarks** | **Ek Sau** (100), **Paanch Sau** (500), **Ek Hazaar** (1,000), **Ek Laakh** (100,000) |

> 💡 **Diya's Tip:** Notice how Hindi numbers from 11 to 18 end in an *-ah* sound (*baarah, pandrah, satrah*), and 19/29 anticipate the next decade with *Un-* (*Unnees* = 20 minus 1, *Untees* = 30 minus 1).

## ⚖️ 2. Core Quantifiers: Thodaa, Bahut, Zyaadaa, Kam, Kuch, Sab

Ye 6 words daily conversation ke backbone hain:

1. **Thodaa / Thodii / Thode** (A little / some — Tamil: *konjam*, French: *un peu*):
    * *"Thodaa paani milegaa?"* (Can I get a little water?)
    * *"Thodaa discount milegaa kyaa?"* (Can I get a little discount?)
1. **Bahut** (Very / a lot / many — Tamil: *romba*, French: *beaucoup*):
    * *"Ye jacket bahut achhii hai."* (This jacket is very good.)
    * *"Toronto mein bahut log hain."* (There are a lot of people in Toronto.)
1. **Zyaadaa** (More / excessive / too much — Tamil: *athigam*, Spanish: *demasiado*):
    * *"Ye price zyaadaa hai."* (This price is too high/excessive.)
    * *"Coffee mein zyaadaa cheeni mat daalna."* (Don't put too much sugar in the coffee.)
1. **Bahut zyaadaa** (Way too much / far too many):
    * *"Arre, ye toh bahut zyaadaa mehengaa hai!"* (Whoa, this is way too expensive!)
1. **Kam** (Less / fewer / reduced — Tamil: *koraivu*, French: *moins*):
    * *"Thodaa kam karo na."* (Reduce it a bit, please.)
    * *"Is cocktail mein ice kam rakhna."* (Keep the ice light in this cocktail.)
1. **Kuch** (Some / a few / indefinite things or people — Tamil: *sila*, French: *quelques*):
    * *"Mujhe market se kuch cheezein khareedni hain."* (I need to buy some things from the market.)
    * *"Kuch log wait kar rahe hain."* (A few people are waiting.)
1. **Sab / Saare** (All / everything / everyone — Tamil: *ellaam*, French: *tout/tous*):
    * *"Sab theek hai."* (Everything is fine.)
    * *"Saare dost pub mein pahunch gaye."* (All the friends reached the pub.)

## 🎯 3. The Power Suffix: "-vaalaa" (The One / Celui-ci)

Hindi ka sabse useful suffix hai **-vaalaa**! Ye adjectives, demonstratives, aur nouns ke aage lag kar specific item ko pinpoint karta hai:

* **With Demonstratives (Pointing at items):**
    * **Ye vaalaa** (This one — masculine / general) $\leftrightarrow$ **Vo vaalaa** (That one — masculine / general)
    * **Ye vaalee** (This one — feminine) $\leftrightarrow$ **Vo vaalee** (That one — feminine)
    * **Ye vaale** (These ones — plural) $\leftrightarrow$ **Vo vaale** (Those ones — plural)
* **With Adjectives (Descriptors):**
    * **Chhotaa vaalaa** (The small one) vs. **Badaa vaalaa** (The big one)
    * **Sastaa vaalaa** (The cheaper one) vs. **Mehengaa vaalaa** (The expensive one)
    * **Blue vaalaa / Nayaa vaalaa** (The blue one / The new one)
* **With Nouns & Professions (People who do / sell X):**
    * **Chai-vaalaa** (Tea vendor), **Auto-vaalaa** (Auto driver), **Doodh-vaalaa** (Milk vendor).

## 🤝 5. Street Bargaining & Negotiation Etiquette

South Asian markets mein bargaining rude nahi, balki ek friendly social dance hai:

1. **Step 1: The Initial Reaction (Express light shock):**
    * *"Arre bhaiyya, ye thodaa mehengaa hai!"* (Oh brother, this is a bit pricey!)
    * *"Bahut zyaadaa bol rahe hain aap."* (You're quoting way too high.)
1. **Step 2: The Polite Squeeze:**
    * *"Thodaa kam kijiye na."* (Please bring it down a bit.)
    * *"Theek rate lagaaiye."* (Quote a fair/reasonable rate.)
    * *"Discount milegaa kyaa?"* (Can I get a discount?)
1. **Step 3: The Counter-Offer:**
    * *"Paanch sau (500) mein doge?"* (Will you give it for 500?)
    * *"Do sau (200) mein final karo."* (Make it 200 final.)
1. **Step 4: The Strategic Walkaway (Power Move):**
    * *"Theek hai, rehne dijiye, main baad mein dekhūngaa."* (Alright, let it be, I will look around later.)
    * *(80% of the time, the shopkeeper will call you back: "Arre aao bhaiyya, le lo!").*

# ❓Quiz


### Concept & Grammar Questions

<details><summary>**Suffix ****`vaalaa`**** gender aur number ke hisaab se kaise modify hota hai, aur Tamil ya French ke kis concept se parallel karta hai?**</summary>

* **Answer:** `vaalaa` masculine singular ke liye use hota hai (*ye vaalaa phone*), `vaalee` feminine singular ke liye (*ye vaalee guitar/jacket*), aur `vaale` masculine plural ya respect ke liye (*ye vaale shoes*). Tamil mein yeh demonstrative suffix *thu* (*intha* $\rightarrow$ *ithu*, *antha* $\rightarrow$ *anthathu*) aur French mein *celui-ci / celle-ci / ceux-ci* ke directly parallel hai.
</details>

<details><summary>**Price poochte waqt ****`kitne kaa hai`**** vs ****`kitne kee hai`**** vs ****`kitne ke hain`**** kab use karte hain?**</summary>

* **Answer:** Postposition `kaa / kee / ke` us target object ke gender aur grammatical number se agree karta hai jiska price poocha jaa raha hai. Masculine singular nouns ke liye `kaa` (*phone kitne kaa hai?*), feminine nouns ke liye `kee` (*coffee / T-shirt kitne kee hai?*), aur plural nouns ke liye `ke` (*ye joote / apples kitne ke hain?*).
</details>

<details><summary>**`Bahut`**** aur ****`Zyaadaa`**** ke semantic nuance mein kya farq hai, aur "way too much" ko Hindi mein kaise combine karte hain?**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Bahut` large quantity ya high degree ko describe karta hai (*"very / a lot"* — e.g. *bahut achhaa*), jabki `Zyaadaa` excessiveness ya comparative superiority ko indicate karta hai (*"more / too much"* — e.g. *zyaadaa cheeni*). Extreme excessiveness ("way too much") express karne ke liye dono ko join karke **Bahut zyaadaa** bolte hain.
</details>

### Translation Questions

<details><summary>**How much is this blue one? (referring to a jacket, feminine)**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Ye blue vaalee kitne kee hai?` — यह ब्लू वाली कितने की है?
<small>Ye (this) + blue vaalee (blue one, fem) + kitne kee (how much, fem agreement) + hai (is)?</small>
</details>

<details><summary>**Brother, this is a bit expensive, please reduce it a little. (polite)**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Bhaiyya, ye thodaa mehengaa hai, thodaa kam kijiye na.` — भैया, यह थोड़ा महँगा है, थोड़ा कम कीजिए ना।
<small>Bhaiyya (brother/vendor) + ye (this) + thodaa (a little) + mehengaa (expensive) + hai (is), thodaa (a little) + kam kijiye na (please reduce).</small>
</details>

<details><summary>**What is the total amount? Do you take card? (polite)**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Total kitnaa huaa? Kyaa aap card lete hain?` — टोटल कितना हुआ? क्या आप कार्ड लेते हैं?
<small>Total kitnaa huaa (how much did total become) + kyaa aap (do you, polite) + card + lete hain (take)?</small>
</details>

<details><summary>**I need to buy some gifts for Shivani before the UK trip. (male speaker)**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Mujhe UK trip se pehle Shivani ke liye kuch gifts khareedne hain.` — मुझे यूके ट्रिप से पहले शिवानी के लिए कुछ गिफ्ट्स खरीदने हैं।
<small>Mujhe (to me) + UK trip se pehle (before UK trip) + Shivani ke liye (for Shivani) + kuch gifts (some gifts) + khareedne hain (have to buy, agreeing with plural gifts).</small>
</details>

<details><summary>**Give me twenty-five dollars for these audio cables. (casual)**</summary>

* **Answer:** `In audio cables ke liye mujhe pachchees dollar do.` — इन ऑडियो केबल्स के लिए मुझे पच्चीस डॉलर दो।
<small>In audio cables ke liye (for these cables, oblique plural) + mujhe (to me) + pachchees (25) + dollar + do (give, casual).</small>
</details>

<details><summary>**I don't have that much cash right now.**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Mere paas abhi utnaa cash nahin hai.` — मेरे पास अभी उतना कैश नहीं है।
<small>Mere paas (with me / in my possession) + abhi (right now) + utnaa cash (that much cash) + nahin hai (is not).</small>
</details>

<details><summary>**Show me that bigger synthesizer. (polite)**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Mujhe vo badaa vaalaa synthesizer dikhaaiye.` — मुझे वो बड़ा वाला सिंथेसाइज़र दिखाइए।
<small>Mujhe (to me) + vo badaa vaalaa (that big one) + synthesizer + dikhaaiye (please show, polite imperative).</small>
</details>

<details><summary>**All friends are waiting at the pub.**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Saare dost pub mein wait kar rahe hain.` — सारे दोस्त पब में वेट कर रहे हैं।
<small>Saare dost (all friends) + pub mein (in the pub) + wait kar rahe hain (are waiting, plural continuous).</small>
</details>

<details><summary>**Can you quote a fair price for this acoustic guitar? (polite)**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Is acoustic guitar ke liye sahi rate lagaaiye na?` — इस अकॉस्टिक गिटार के लिए सही रेट लगाइए ना?
<small>Is guitar ke liye (for this guitar, oblique 'is') + sahi rate (fair price) + lagaaiye na (please apply/quote)?</small>
</details>

<details><summary>**Don't buy that expensive one; this one is very good. (casual, addressing friend)**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Vo mehengaa vaalaa mat khareedo; ye vaalaa bahut achhaa hai.` — वो महँगा वाला मत ख़रीदो; यह वाला बहुत अच्छा है।
<small>Vo mehengaa vaalaa (that expensive one) + mat khareedo (don't buy, casual negative imperative) + ye vaalaa (this one) + bahut achhaa hai (is very good).</small>
</details>
