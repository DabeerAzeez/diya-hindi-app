# Lesson A1-04: Obligation, Compulsion & Permission


# 🌟Summary

Is lesson mein hum teen important concepts seekhenge: **Obligation & Habitual Compulsion (*****Padtaa hai*****)**, **Strong Necessity / Personal Obligation (*****Infinitive + Hai / Padegaa*****)**, aur **Permission / Prohibition (*****Saknaa***** / *****Manaa hai*****)**. Tamil ke `-a vendum` ya `-a vendiyirukku` pattern ki tarah, Hindi mein bhi jab external compulsion ya daily routine obligation express karna hota hai, toh subject dative pronoun (`Mujhe`, `Aapko`, etc.) leta hai aur verb frame change ho jaata hai.

| **Structure** | **Grammar Frame** | **Usage / Context** | **Tamil / Polyglot Anchor** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Internal Obligation / Plan** | `Subject(Dative) + Verb(Inf) + Hai` | Simple "have to / must" (personal choice/plan) | Tamil: *Enakku poga vendum* |
| **Habitual / External Compulsion** | `Subject(Dative) + Verb(Inf) + Padtaa hai` | Daily forced chore / "no choice but to" | Tamil: *Enakku poga vendiyirukku* |
| **Future Compulsion** | `Subject(Dative) + Verb(Inf) + Padegaa` | "Will have to" (future obligation) | Tamil: *Poga vendiyirukkum* |
| **Permission (Can / May)** | `Subject + Verb(Stem) + Saktaa hoon / Sakte hain` | Asking for / giving permission | "May I / Can I" |
| **Prohibition** | `Verb(Inf) + Manaa hai` | Strict rule / "Forbidden" | Tamil: *Koodaathu* / Strict "Not allowed" |


# 📘Lesson


## 📌 1. Internal Obligation: `Mujhe [Verb]-naa Hai`

Jab aapko koi kaam simply karna hai (aapka plan ya personal obligation hai), toh subject hamesha dative pronoun (`Mujhe`, `Tumhe`, `Aapko`, `Use`) ban jaata hai.

$$\text{Subject (Dative)} + \text{Infinitive Verb (-naa)} + \text{Hai}$$

* **Mujhe jaanaa hai.** (I have to go.)
* **Mujhe Shivani se milnaa hai.** (I have to meet Shivani.)
* **Aapko kab nikalnaa hai?** (When do you have to leave?)

## 🔄 2. Habitual & External Compulsion: `Padtaa Hai` vs `Padegaa`

Agar koi kaam aap apni marzi se nahi, balki routine, rule, ya majboori ki wajah se karte hain, toh `padtaa hai` use hota hai. Future compulsion ke liye `padegaa` use karte hain.

$$\text{Subject (Dative)} + \text{Infinitive Verb (-naa)} + \text{Padtaa hai / Padegaa}$$

* **Mujhe daily 9 AM work start karna padtaa hai.** (I have to start work at 9 AM daily.)
* **Use daily travel karna padtaa hai.** (He/She has to travel daily.)
* **Kal mujhe extra time kaam karna padegaa.** (Tomorrow I will have to work overtime.)

## 🚪 3. Permission & Polite Requests: `Kya main... saktaa hoon?`

Permission maangne ke liye hum ability verb `saknaa` ko subjunctive ya polite present form mein use karte hain.

$$\text{Kya} + \text{Subject} + \text{Verb Stem} + \text{Saktaa/Saktii hoon?}$$

* **Kya main andar aa saktaa hoon?** (May / Can I come in?)
* **Kya hum yahaan baith sakte hain?** (Can we sit here?)

## 🚫 4. Prohibition & Rules: `[Verb]-naa Manaa Hai`

Kahin par koi rule ya strict ban batane ke liye infinitive verb ke saath `manaa hai` lagate hain.

$$\text{Infinitive Verb (-naa)} + \text{Manaa hai}$$

* **Yahaan photo lenaa manaa hai.** (Taking photos is prohibited here.)
* **Andar aanaa manaa hai.** (Entering is not allowed.)

# ❓Quiz


### Concept & Grammar Questions

<details><summary>`Mujhe kaam karna hai` aur `Mujhe kaam karna padtaa hai` mein core difference kya hai?</summary>

* **Answer:** `Mujhe kaam karna hai` ek simple intention/personal obligation show karta hai ("I have to work"), jabki `Mujhe kaam karna padtaa hai` ek habitual/external compulsion show karta hai ("I am forced / required by routine to work").
</details>

<details><summary>Jab hum obligation express karte hain (`jaanaa hai` ya `padtaa hai`), toh subject pronoun `Main` se `Mujhe` kyun ban jaata hai?</summary>

* **Answer:** Kyunki obligation structures dative subjects use karte hain, bilkul Tamil ke dative marker (*ukku*, jaise *Enakku vendum*) ki tarah.
</details>

<details><summary>Future compulsion express karne ke liye formula kya hoga?</summary>

* **Answer:** `Subject (Dative) + Infinitive Verb (-naa) + Padegaa` (jaise *Mujhe train pakadna padegaa*).
</details>

<details><summary>Agar kisi public place par smoking strictly prohibited hai, toh aap Hinglish/Hindi mein rule kaise bolenge?</summary>

* **Answer:** `Yahaan smoking karna manaa hai` (Infinitive verb + `manaa hai`).
</details>


### Translation Questions

<details><summary>**I have to prepare for my UK trip.**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Mujhe meri UK trip ke liye prepare karna hai. — मुझे मेरी यूके ट्रिप के लिए प्रिपेयर करना है।`
</details>

<details><summary>**Every day I have to attend morning team meetings.**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Mujhe daily morning team meetings attend karna padtaa hai. — मुझे डेली मॉर्निंग टीम मीटिंग्स अटेंड करना पड़ता है।`
</details>

<details><summary>**Tomorrow we will have to leave early for the station.**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Kal humein station ke liye jaldi nikalna padegaa. — कल हमें स्टेशन के लिए जल्दी निकलना पड़ेगा।`
</details>

<details><summary>**Can I ask you a question?**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Kya main aapse ek question pooch saktaa hoon? — क्या मैं आपसे एक क्वेश्चन पूछ सकता हूँ?`
</details>

<details><summary>**Parking is not allowed here.**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Yahaan car park karna manaa hai. — यहाँ कार पार्क करना मना है।`
</details>

<details><summary>**Shivani has to coordinate daily activities at the care home.**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Shivani ko care home mein daily activities coordinate karna padtaa hai. — शिवानी को केयर होम में डेली एक्टिविटीज़ कोऑर्डिनेट करना पड़ता है।`
</details>

<details><summary>**Aapko weekend par kaam karna pasand nahi hai, lekin karna padtaa hai.**</summary>

* **Answer:** You don't like working on weekends, but you have to do it (external compulsion).
</details>

<details><summary>**Kya main abhi ghar jaa saktaa hoon?**</summary>

* **Answer:** May / Can I go home right now?
</details>

<details><summary>**Humein kal subah jaldi ready hona hai.**</summary>

* **Answer:** We have to get ready early tomorrow morning.
</details>

<details><summary>**Yahaan loud music play karna manaa hai.**</summary>

* **Answer:** Playing loud music is prohibited here.
</details>
