# Lesson A0-02: Present Continuous & Present Habitual (Doing vs. Does)


# 🌟 Summary

Lesson 2 focuses on narrating actions in the present tense, distinguishing between continuous actions happening right now (-ing) and habitual everyday routines (-es/-s)[cite: 2]. For a male speaker, continuous actions use the verb stem paired with `-rahaa hoon`, while routines use the stem paired with `-taa hoon`[cite: 1, 2]. This aligns directly with Tamil's distinct verb suffixes for current actions vs. routine habits[cite: 1, 2].


# 📘 Lesson


## 💡 Key Grammar Formulas & Rules

* **Present Continuous Formula:**
MATH: \text{Subject} + \text{Object} + \text{Verb Stem} + \text{rahaa/rahii/rahe} + \text{"To Be" Verb}
* **Present Habitual Formula:**
MATH: \text{Subject} + \text{Object} + \text{Verb Stem} + \text{taa/tii/te} + \text{"To Be" Verb}
* **Male Speaker Core Rule:** As a male speaker, you lock in **`rahaa`** for continuous actions happening right now and **`taa`** for general daily habits and routines[cite: 1, 2].

## 📌 Continuous vs. Habitual Breakdown

* **Present Continuous (Right Now / In Progress):**
    * Expresses actions currently underway (e.g., *working*, *watching*, *playing*)[cite: 2].
    * **Example:** `Main abhi kaam kar rahaa hoon. — मैं अभी काम कर रहा हूँ.`[cite: 2]
<small>Main (I) | abhi (right now) | kaam kar (do work - stem) | rahaa (doing - male) | hoon (am)</small>
* **Present Habitual (Routines / Habits / General Truths):**
    * Expresses ongoing habits, regular schedules, or general facts[cite: 2].
    * **Example:** `Main daily Toronto me kaam karta hoon. — मैं daily Toronto में काम करता हूँ.`
<small>Main (I) | daily (every day) | Toronto me (in Toronto) | kaam kar (do work - stem) | taa (do - male) | hoon (am)</small>

## 🔗 Polyglot Anchors & Gender Mechanics

* **Tamil Parallel:**
    * Continuous (*doing right now*) maps to Tamil `t-irukken` (e.g., *panneettu irukken* $\rightarrow$ *kar rahaa hoon*)[cite: 1, 2].
    * Habitual (*do daily*) maps to Tamil habitual present verbs (e.g., *panren* $\rightarrow$ *karta hoon*)[cite: 1, 2].
* **Masculine Lock:** Since the learner is male, present action stems always end in `rahaa` or `taa` when speaking about himself, regardless of whether the listener is male or female[cite: 1, 2].

## 🎯 Real-World Applications & Catch-Up Scenarios

* **Daily Life & Hobbies:** Describing routine activities like playing guitar, piano, gaming, or working in AI/software[cite: 2, 4].
    * **Example:** `Main daily evening me piano practice karta hoon. — मैं daily evening में piano practice करता हूँ.`[cite: 2]
<small>Main (I) | daily (every day) | evening me (in the evening) | piano (piano) | practice kar (do practice - stem) | taa (do - male) | hoon (am)</small>
* **Catching Up with Friends (Shivani):** Asking friends about their current status vs. their daily routine[cite: 2, 4].
    * **Example:** `Tum Norwich me kyaa kar rahe ho? — तुम Norwich में क्या कर रहे हो?`[cite: 2]
<small>Tum (You) | Norwich me (in Norwich) | kyaa (what) | kar (do - stem) | rahe (doing - plural/casual) | ho (are)</small>

# ❓ Quiz


### Concept & Grammar Questions

<details><summary>**What is the key structural difference between saying you are doing something right now vs. doing something routinely in Hindi?**</summary>

* **Answer:** Continuous actions (right now) use the **Verb Stem + ****`rahaa/rahii/rahe`**** + To Be**, whereas habitual actions (routines) use the **Verb Stem + ****`taa/tii/te`**** + To Be**[cite: 2].
</details>

<details><summary>**As a male speaker, which verb suffixes must you use for yourself when talking about actions?**</summary>

* **Answer:** `rahaa hoon` for actions in progress right now, and `taa hoon` for routine habits[cite: 1, 2].
</details>


### Translation Questions

<details><summary>**I am working right now.**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Main abhi kaam kar rahaa hoon. — मैं अभी काम कर रहा हूँ.`[cite: 2]
<small>Main (I) | abhi (right now) | kaam kar (do work) | rahaa (doing - male) | hoon (am)</small>
</details>

<details><summary>**I live in Toronto.**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Main Toronto me rahta hoon. — मैं Toronto में रहता हूँ.`[cite: 2]
<small>Main (I) | Toronto me (in Toronto) | rah (live/stay - stem) | taa (do - male) | hoon (am)</small>
</details>

<details><summary>**What are you doing? (Casual to a friend)**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Tum kyaa kar rahe ho? — तुम क्या कर रहे हो?`[cite: 2]
<small>Tum (You) | kyaa (what) | kar (do - stem) | rahe (doing - male plural/casual) | ho (are)</small>
</details>

<details><summary>**I watch movies on weekends.**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Main weekend par movies dekh-taa hoon. — मैं weekend पर movies देखता हूँ.`
<small>Main (I) | weekend par (on weekends) | movies (movies) | dekh (watch - stem) | taa (do - male) | hoon (am)</small>
</details>

<details><summary>**Are you coming today? (Formal)**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Kyaa aap aaj aa rahe haim? — क्या आप आज आ रहे हैं?`[cite: 2]
<small>Kyaa (Question marker) | aap (you - formal) | aaj (today) | aa (come - stem) | rahe (doing - respect) | haim (are)</small>
</details>

<details><summary>**I speak English and French.**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Main English aur French bolta hoon. — मैं English और French बोलता हूँ.`[cite: 2]
<small>Main (I) | English (English) | aur (and) | French (French) | bol (speak - stem) | taa (do - male) | hoon (am)</small>
</details>

<details><summary>**Main abhi music listen kar rahaa hoon.**</summary>

* **Answer:** I am listening to music right now.
</details>

<details><summary>**Aap kyaa karte haim?**</summary>

* **Answer:** What do you do? (Formal/Career context)[cite: 2]
</details>

<details><summary>**Shivani Norwich me care home me kaam karti hai.**</summary>

* **Answer:** Shivani works in a care home in Norwich[cite: 4].
</details>

<details><summary>**Hum daily talk karte haim.**</summary>

* **Answer:** We talk daily.
</details>
