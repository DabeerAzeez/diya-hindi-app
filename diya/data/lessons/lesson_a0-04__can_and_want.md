# Lesson A0-04: Can and Want


# 🌟 Summary

In **Lesson 3**, we unlocked how to express your goals, needs, preferences, and abilities using high-yield modal grammar frames. You learned the mechanics of expressing what you **want to do** (`-naa chaahtaa hoon`), what you **can do** (`saktaa hoon`), and what you **need or like** using indirect subject frames (`Mujhe... chaahiye` / `Mujhe... pasand hai`).


### Core Modal Patterns Overview

| **Expression Type** | **Formula / Structure** | **Example (Male Speaker)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Want to (Action)** | Verb Infinitive (`-naa`) + `chaahtaa hoon` | *Main Shivani se milnaa chaahtaa hoon.* |
| **Can / Able to** | Verb Stem (drop `-naa`) + `saktaa hoon` | *Main piano play kar saktaa hoon.* |
| **Need / Want (Item)** | `Mujhe` + Item + `chaahiye` | *Mujhe thodaa time chaahiye.* |
| **Like (Item/Action)** | `Mujhe` + Item/Action + `pasand hai` | *Mujhe ye place pasand hai.* |


# 📘 Lesson 3 Key Grammar Reference


## 🎯 1. Desires: Wanting to do an Action

To express wanting to perform an action, attach `chaahtaa hoon` (for male speakers) directly after the full verb infinitive (ending in `-naa`).

MATH: \text{Subject} + \text{Object} + \text{Verb (-naa)} + \text{chaahtaa hoon}

* **Example:** I want to speak Hindi.
    * `Main Hindi bolnaa chaahtaa hoon. — मैं हिंदी बोलना चाहता हूँ।`

## ⚡ 2. Ability: Expressing "Can"

To express ability, attach `saktaa hoon` (for male speakers) directly to the **bare verb stem** (strip off the `-naa` suffix).

MATH: \text{Subject} + \text{Object} + \text{Verb Stem} + \text{saktaa hoon}

* **Example:** I can help.
    * `Main help kar saktaa hoon. — मैं help कर सकता हूँ।`

## 💡 3. Indirect Subjects: Needs & Preferences

In Hindi, needs and likes use an **indirect subject pattern** where the target person takes the dative suffix `ko` (or standard indirect pronouns like `Mujhe` for "to me").

* **Desires (****`chaahiye`****):**
    * $$\text{Indirect Subject (Mujhe/Aapko)} + \text{Noun/Verb (infinitive)} + \text{chaahiye}$$
    * *Example:* I need coffee. $\rightarrow$ `Mujhe coffee chaahiye. — मुझे coffee चाहिए।`
    * *Example: *I need to go. $\rightarrow$ `Mujhe jaana chaahiye`
* **Likes (****`pasand hai`****):**
    * $$\text{Indirect Subject (Mujhe/Aapko)} + \text{Noun/Verb (infinitive)} + \text{pasand hai}$$
    * *Example:* I like this place. $\rightarrow$ `Mujhe ye place pasand hai. — मुझे यह place पसंद है।`
    * *Example: I like to run. *`Mujhe daudana pasand hai` 

# ❓ Quiz


### Concept & Grammar Questions

<details><summary>**What is the structural difference between using ****`chaahtaa hoon`**** vs. ****`saktaa hoon`**** with an action verb?**</summary>

* **Answer:** `chaahtaa hoon` requires the **full infinitive** form of the verb (ending in `naa`), whereas `saktaa hoon` attaches directly to the **bare verb stem** (dropping the `naa`).
</details>

<details><summary>**Why do we say ****`Mujhe coffee chaahiye`**** instead of ****`Main coffee chaahiye`****?**</summary>

* **Answer:** `chaahiye` uses an indirect subject construction where the sentence literally translates to "To me, coffee is needed," requiring `Mujhe` ("to me") rather than `Main` ("I").
</details>


### Translation Questions

<details><summary>**I want to meet you.**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Main aap se milnaa chaahtaa hoon. — मैं आप से मिलना चाहता हूँ।`
</details>

<details><summary>**I need some time.**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Mujhe thodaa time chaahiye. — मुझे थोड़ा time चाहिए।`
</details>

<details><summary>**Can you help me?**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Kyaa aap merī help kar sakte haim? — क्या आप मेरी help कर सकते हैं?`
</details>

<details><summary>**I like this place.**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Mujhe ye place pasand hai. — मुझे यह place पसंद है।`
</details>

<details><summary>**I don't know.**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Mujhe nahīm pataa. — मुझे नहीं पता।`
</details>

<details><summary>**I can speak a little Hindi.**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Main thodī Hindi bol saktaa hoon. — मैं थोड़ी Hindi बोल सकता हूँ।`
</details>

<details><summary>**Do you want to eat?**</summary>

* **Answer:** `Kyaa tum khaanaa chaahte ho? — क्या तुम खाना चाहते हो?`
</details>

<details><summary>**Should you eat that?**</summary>

* `Tumhe vo khaana chaahiye?`
</details>

<details><summary>**`Main daily evening me piano practice kar saktaa hoon.`**</summary>

* **Answer:** I can practice piano every day in the evening.
</details>

<details><summary>**`Kyaa tum coffee peenaa chaahte ho?`**</summary>

* **Answer:** Do you want to drink coffee?
</details>
