# Lesson A1-01: Imperatives & Giving Directions


# 🌟Summary

Is lesson mein hum **Imperatives (Commands/Requests)** aur **Directions/Spatial Postpositions** seekhenge! Imperatives direct verbs hote hain jo instructions, advice, ya raste batane ke liye use hote hain.

* **Tum (Casual/Friendly):** Verb stem + $-o$ (e.g., *aao*, *baitho*, *bataao*)
* **Aap (Polite/Formal):** Verb stem + $-iye$ (e.g., *aaiye*, *baithiye*, *bataaiye*)
* **Negative Commands:** *Mat* (preferred/direct) ya *Na* use hota hai verb se pehle (e.g., *tension mat lo*)
Spatial directions bilkul Tamil aur English ki tarah logical hain, par Hindi mein postpositions landmark ke baad aate hain.


$$
\text{Verb Stem} + \text{-o} \longrightarrow \text{Tum Imperative (Casual)}
$$


$$
\text{Verb Stem} + \text{-iye} \longrightarrow \text{Aap Imperative (Polite)}
$$


$$
\text{Mat} + \text{Imperative Verb} \longrightarrow \text{Negative Command}
$$


# 📘Lesson


## 🗣️ Tum vs. Aap Imperatives

* **Tum level (Informal/Friends):**
    * *Suno* (Listen)
    * *Dekho* (Look)
    * *Ruko* (Stop/Wait)
    * *Yahaam aao* (Come here)
* **Aap level (Polite/Respectful):**
    * *Suniye* (Please listen)
    * *Dekhiye* (Please look)
    * *Rukiye* (Please wait)
    * *Yahaam aaiye* (Please come here)
* **Irregular Stems (Crucial):**
    * *Lenaa* (To take) → *Lo* (Tum) / *Lijiye* (Aap)
    * *Denaa* (To give) → *Do* (Tum) / *Dijiye* (Aap)
    * *Karnaa* (To do) → *Karo* (Tum) / *Kijiye* (Aap)
    * *Peenaa* (To drink) → *Piyo* (Tum) / *Pijiye* (Aap)

## 🚫 Negative Imperatives (Mat vs. Na)

* Direct prohibition ke liye **Mat** use karo:
    * *Tension mat lo!* (Don't stress/take tension!)
    * *Late mat ho.* (Don't be late.)
    * *Wahaam mat jaao.* (Don't go there.)

## 🧭 Directional Vocabulary & Spatial Postpositions

* **Directions:**
    * *Seedha* (Straight)
    * *Daayeim* / *Right* (Right)
    * *Baayeim* / *Left* (Left)
    * *Aage* (Ahead/Forward)
    * *Peeche* (Behind/Back)
* **Spatial Postpositions:**
    * *Ke saamne* (In front of / opposite)
    * *Ke paas* (Near)
    * *Ke andar* (Inside)
    * *Ke baahar* (Outside)
    * *Ke peeche* (Behind)
* **Turn Verbs:**
    * *Mudo* / *Mudiye* (Turn)
    * *Right mudo* (Turn right)

# ❓Quiz


### Concept & Grammar Questions

<details><summary>**Casual (*****tum*****) aur polite (*****aap*****) imperatives banane ka formula kya hai?**</summary>

* **Answer:** *Tum* ke liye verb stem mein $-o$ add karte hain, aur *aap* ke liye stem mein
* $-iye$ add karte hain.
</details>

<details><summary>**Hindi mein negative command/request dene ke liye kaunsa word verb se pehle use hota hai?**</summary>

* **Answer:** *Mat* (e.g., *Mat jaao*, *Tension mat lo*).
</details>

<details><summary>**"Care home ke saamne" phrase mein postposition kya hai aur iska meaning kya hai?**</summary>

* **Answer:** Postposition *ke saamne* hai, jiska meaning "in front of / opposite to" hota hai.
</details>


### Translation Questions

<details><summary>**Take right from the station and go straight.** *(informal)*</summary>

* **Answer:** `Station se right mudo aur seedha jaao.`
* Station (station) | se (from) | right (right) | mudo (turn) | aur (and) | seedha (straight) | jaao (go)
</details>

<details><summary>**Please don't worry.** *(polite)*</summary>

* **Answer:** `Chinta mat kijiye.` / `Tension mat lijiye.`
* Chinta/Tension (worry/tension) | mat (don't) | kijiye/lijiye (please do/take)
</details>

<details><summary>**Pub ke saamne ruko.**</summary>

* **Answer:** Stop/Wait in front of the pub.
</details>

<details><summary>**Please come inside and sit.** *(polite)*</summary>

* **Answer:** `Andar aaiye aur baithiye.`
* Andar (inside) | aaiye (please come) | aur (and) | baithiye (please sit)
</details>

<details><summary>**Aage se left lo.**</summary>

* **Answer:** Take a left from ahead.
</details>
