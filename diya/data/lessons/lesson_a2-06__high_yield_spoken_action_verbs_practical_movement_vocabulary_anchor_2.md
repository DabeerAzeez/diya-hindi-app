# Lesson A2-06: High-Yield Spoken Action Verbs & Practical Movement 🏷️ (Vocabulary Anchor 2)

# 🌟Summary

Daily life, travel, aur spontaneous meetup situations mein physical objects ko handle karna aur dynamic movement describe karna essential hotaa hai. Is lesson mein hum high-yield physical action verbs (*Rakhnaa, Uthaanaa, Chhodnaa, Pakadnaa, Sambhaalnaa*) master karenge.

### 🧠 Polyglot Structural Anchors

* **Intransitive vs Transitive Movement Pairs:**
Spanish (*quedarse vs dejar*) aur Tamil (*nillu vs niruthu*) ki tarah Hindi mein *Ruknaa* (to pause/stop oneself) vs *Roknaa* (to stop something externally) pair hota hai.

* **Object Holding Idioms:**
Tamil *pidi* (to catch/hold) Hindi *Pakadnaa* se perfectly match karta hai.

### 📐 Core Mathematical Formulas

$$\text{Intransitive (Self)}: \text{Ruknaa (to stop)}, \text{Girnaa (to fall)}, \text{Bachnaa (to survive)}$$

$$\text{Transitive (Caused)}: \text{Roknaa (to stop sth)}, \text{Giraanaa (to drop)}, \text{Bachaanaa (to save)}$$

# 📘Lesson

## 📦 1. Handling Objects & Physical Belongings

* **Rakhnaa:** *Yeh bag table par rakh do.* (Put this bag on the table.)

* **Uthaanaa:** *Heavy box mat uthaao.* (Don't lift the heavy box.)

* **Chhodnaa:** *Mujhe station par chhod denaa.* (Drop me off at the station.)

* **Pakadnaa:** *Meraa phone pakdo ek minute.* (Hold my phone for a second.)

## 🚶 2. Transit, Arrival & Searching

* **Pahunchnaa:** *Main 10 minutes mein pahunchūngaa.* (I will arrive in 10 minutes.)

* **Dhoondhnaa:** *Main apnii keys dhoondh rahaa hoon.* (I am looking for my keys.)

* **Milnaa:** *Keys mil gayiin!* (The keys were found!)

* **Chalaanaa:** *Gaadi dhyaan se chalaao.* (Drive the car carefully.)

## 🛡️ 3. Managing & Taking Care

* **Sambhaalnaa:** *Fikar mat karo, main sab sambhaal loongaa.* (Don't worry, I will manage / handle everything.)

* *Yeh documents sambhaal kar rakhnaa.* (Keep these documents safely.)

---

## 🎭 Dialogue Example

* **Shivani:** *"Station pahunch kar mujhe call karnaa, main tumhe pick karne aa jaaoongii."*
* **You:** *"Main train mein baith gayaa hoon aur 4 baje tak pahunchūngaa. Apnaa phone paas rakhnaa!"*
* **Shivani:** *"Bilkul! Luggage sambhaal kar uthaanaa, platform par bheed ho saktii hai."*
* **You:** *"Haan, fikar mat karo, main easily manage kar loongaa."*

# ❓Quiz

### Concept & Grammar Questions

1. **'Ruknaa' aur 'Roknaa' mein kyaa conceptual farq hai?**
    * **Answer:** *Ruknaa* intransitive hai jisme subject khud thahar jaata hai (*Hum red light par ruke* = We stopped), jabki *Roknaa* transitive hai jisme subject kisi doosri cheez ko rokta hai (*Police ne car ko rokaa* = Police stopped the car).
1. **Searching action 'Dhoondhnaa' aur finding outcome 'Milnaa' mein grammatical structure kaise badaltaa hai?**
    * **Answer:** *Dhoondhnaa* active transitive verb hai jo subject dwara ki jaati hai (*Main phone dhoondh rahaa hoon*), jabki *Milnaa* dative/passive result hai jisme object mil jaata hai (*Mujhe phone mil gayaa*).

### Translation Questions

1. **Please drop me off near the Norwich city library. (polite)**
    * **Answer:** `Kripya mujhe Norwich city library ke paas chhod dijiye.` — कृपया मुझे नॉर्विच सिटी लाइब्रेरी के पास छोड़ दीजिए।
    * [Kripya mujhe + Norwich city library ke paas + chhod dijiye (please drop off)].
1. **Hold my jacket for a moment while I tie my shoes. (casual)**
    * **Answer:** `Ek minute meri jacket pakdo jab tak main apne joote baandh loon.` — एक मिनट मेरी जैकेट पकड़ो जब तक मैं अपने जूते बाँध लूँ।
    * [Ek minute meri jacket pakdo (hold my jacket) + jab tak (while) + main apne joote baandh loon].
1. **I was looking for my guitar picks everywhere and finally found them. (informal, m)**
    * **Answer:** `Main har jagah apne guitar picks dhoondh rahaa thaa aur aakhirkaar mil gaye.` — मैं हर जगह अपने गिटार पिक्स ढूँढ रहा था और आखिरकार मिल गए।
    * [Main har jagah + apne guitar picks dhoondh rahaa thaa + aur aakhirkaar mil gaye].
1. **Don't worry about the dinner preparations, I will handle everything. (informal, m)**
    * **Answer:** `Dinner preparations ki chintaa mat karo, main sab sambhaal loongaa.` — डिनर प्रिपरेशन्स की चिंता मत करो, मैं सब संभाल लूँगा।
    * [Dinner preparations ki chintaa mat karo + main sab sambhaal loongaa].
1. **What time will your train reach the central station? (polite)**
    * **Answer:** `Aapki train kitne baje central station pahunchegii?` — आपकी ट्रेन कितने बजे सेंट्रल स्टेशन पहुँचेगी?
    * [Aapki train + kitne baje + central station pahunchegii?].
