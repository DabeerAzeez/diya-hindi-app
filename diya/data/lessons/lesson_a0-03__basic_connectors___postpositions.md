# Lesson A0-03: Basic Connectors & Postpositions

Here is a summary table of the core basic connectors and postpositions, mapping them directly from Romanized Hindi to English and Tamil:

| **Romanized Hindi** | **English** | **Tamil Equivalent** | **Hinglish Example** | **English Translation** | **Romanized Tamil Translation** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Me** | In / Inside / At | **-la / -ulle** (இல் / உள்ள) | *Main office me hoon.* | I am in the office. | *Naan office-la irukken.* |
| **Par** | On / At | **mela / -la** (மேல) | *Mera phone table par hai.* | My phone is on the table. | *En phone table mela irukku.* |
| **Se** | From / With / By | **-ilirundhu / -ala** (இருந்து / ஆல) | *Vo Toronto se hai.* | She is from Toronto. | *Ava Toronto-lirundhu irukkaa.* |
| **Ke saath** | With (a person/thing) | **kooda / -oda** (கூட) | *Main Shivani ke saath hoon.* | I am with Shivani. | *Naan Shivani kooda irukken.* |
| **Ke liye** | For | **kaaga / -ukku** (காக) | *Main trip ke liye ready hoon.* | I am ready for the trip. | *Naan trip-kkaaga ready-aa irukken.* |
| **Ke paas** | Near / To have | **kitta** (கிட்ட) | *Mere paas ek guitar hai.* | I have a guitar. | *En kitta oru guitar irukku.* |
| **Ke baare me** | About | **patthi** | *Mujhe October reunion ke baare me baat karnaa chahiye* | I want to talk about the October reunion. | *Naan October reunion paththi pesa vaendum.* |
| **Lekin / Par / ** | But / However | **Aanaal / Aanaa** (ஆனால்) | *Main busy hoon, lekin call karunga.* | I am busy, but I will call. | *Naan busy-aa irukken, aanaa call panren.* |
| **Isliye** | That's why / So | **Adhanaala** (அதனால) | *Main busy thaa, isliye nahi aaya.* | I was busy, that's why I didn't come. | *Naan busy-aa irundhen, adhanaala varala.* |
| **Kyunki** | Because | **Enaa / Yen-naa** (ஏன்னா) | *Main Hindi sikh rahaa hoon kyunki...* | I am learning Hindi because... | *Naan Hindi kathukiren, yen-naa...* |
| **Vaise** | By the way / Actually | **Aamaa / Vaasthavathula** (ஆமா / உண்மையில்) | *Vaise, tum kahaan ho?* | By the way, where are you? | *Aamaa, nee engae irukka?* |
| **Ki (Conjunction)** | That | -nnu / endru (என்று / -ன்னு) | Mujhe pata hai ki tum busy ho. | I know that you are busy. | Enakku theriyum nee busy-nnu. |
| **Kee / Ki (Possessive)** | Of / 's (Feminine) | -oda / udaiya (உடைய / -ஓட) | Ye Shivani ki book hai. | This is Shivani's book. | Idhu Shivani-oda book. |
